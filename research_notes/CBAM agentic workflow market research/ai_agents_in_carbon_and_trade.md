# AI Agents / Agentic Workflows in Carbon Accounting, ESG, Trade Compliance and Regulatory Reporting (state as of Oct 2026)

Research scope note: about 22 search/fetch calls. Most evidence is vendor-authored (blogs, press releases). Independent accuracy evidence exists mainly for HS/HTS classification (academic benchmarks). Two primary press releases (Persefoni on BusinessWire and ESG News) returned HTTP 403, so the Persefoni details come from search-result summaries only. Treat every vendor metric below as self-reported unless marked independent.

## 1. Carbon/ESG platforms: which agent features are real, and what do they do?

### Takeaway
Agentic features have shipped at the leading carbon platforms (Watershed, Sweep, Persefoni, Greenly, Salesforce). They cluster around a few tasks: (a) matching activity/spend/material data to emission factors, (b) cleaning and standardising data, (c) natural-language analytics over a governed ledger, (d) filling questionnaires and drafting disclosures, and (e) "outcome-to-task" orchestration across the platform. None of the serious vendors claims the LLM does the emissions arithmetic. Every one of them markets traceability back to a deterministic ledger or calculation lineage. I found no commercial "CBAM agent" or "CBAM copilot" product: the CBAM vendors offer AI chatbots and data-gap detection, and the only agentic CBAM declaration builder I found is a hackathon project.

### Cited Findings
**Watershed (most detailed public disclosure)**
- Its emission-factor auto-mapper uses two agents. A Mapper Agent matches messy procurement text to LCI databases such as ecoinvent using RAG plus company context like industry. A separate Judge Agent scores each match without seeing the Mapper's reasoning, "to prevent overconfidence," and low-quality or ambiguous matches go to human review with justifications attached. Blog dated 6 Mar 2026. — [Watershed benchmark blog](https://watershed.com/blog/how-we-benchmark-and-measure-the-accuracy-of-watershed-ai-for-emissions-factor-mapping)
- Reported accuracy: 98.9% "defensible mappings" on the Amazon Parakeet benchmark (275 mappings, mostly food). It falls to 90.7% on an expanded 1,039-material set that adds production data and edge cases such as foreign-language names and abbreviations. When the Judge rates a match high quality, the error rate is about 0.3%. Repeatability is near 100% where one unambiguous factor exists. No comparison against other models is given, and Watershed concedes "no single objective ground truth" exists. — [Watershed benchmark blog](https://watershed.com/blog/how-we-benchmark-and-measure-the-accuracy-of-watershed-ai-for-emissions-factor-mapping)
- The 2026 release says AI matches scope 3 data to supplier information and emission factors as *suggested mappings you review and adjust before generating your footprint*. — [Watershed 2026 measurement release](https://watershed.com/blog/2026-measurement-release)
- Named shipped features: a PDF scanner for utility bills, invoices and supplier documents; data-cleaning agents for unit conversion, date standardisation, country-code mapping, duplicate detection and missing values, "with documented assumptions and flagged edge cases"; multi-framework report mapping across CSRD, ISSB, TCFD, CDP, SB 253 and SB 261; "hallucination checks on every AI output," calculation lineage and changelogs. — [Watershed, "Sustainability AI in 2026" (10 Jun 2026)](https://watershed.com/blog/sustainability-ai-in-2026-what-works-what-doesnt-and-why-the-difference-matters)

### Inferences
- The defensible pattern across the market is "LLM at the messy edges, deterministic core." Agents handle classification, mapping, cleaning and document parsing, and propose values. A ledger or calculation engine computes the numbers, and humans approve. This matches carbonTraceAI's architecture, a deterministic CBAM calc engine, which is an advantage to market explicitly.
- Watershed's Mapper/Judge separation with blind scoring and a confidence-based human-review queue is the most concrete, copyable design pattern found. For CBAM it would apply to mapping ERP item masters to CN codes, mapping fuels and precursors to default values, and mapping production routes.
- The CBAM niche has no shipped agentic product: incumbents have chatbots and "AI gap detection." A non-EU-exporter-side agent (operator side, preparing installation data for the CBAM Operators Portal or for importer requests) looks like white space. This is an inference from absence of evidence, not proof.
- Vendor metrics are self-reported, use bespoke definitions ("defensible" rather than exact), and are sometimes internally inconsistent (Watershed's 65-80% vs 80-90%).

## 2. Trade compliance and customs: HS/CN classification agents and reported accuracy

### Takeaway
HS classification is the most rigorously benchmarked area, and the independent numbers are sobering. Exact 10-digit accuracy for frontier LLMs is 13.5-25% on U.S. HTS (ATLAS). A fine-tuned 70B model reaches 40% at 10 digits and 57.5% at 6 digits. A 2024 benchmark put Zonos at about 44% and Avalara's AI-assisted human workflow at about 80%. Vendors claim 90-99%, with no published methodology. The most credible agentic product move is Altana's acquisition of Cervo AI (July 2026), which gives it end-to-end agentic customs-entry writing.

### Cited Findings
- **ATLAS benchmark** (U.S. HTS, built from CBP CROSS rulings, 200 held-out products): GPT-5-Thinking got 25% of 10-digit codes exactly right and Gemini-2.5-Pro-Thinking got 13.5%. A fine-tuned LLaMA-3.3-70B ("Atlas") reached 40% at 10 digits and 57.5% at 6 digits, and can be self-hosted for privacy. — [arXiv 2509.18400](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.18400); [HF papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2509.18400)
- **2024 commercial-tool benchmark** (103 items vs U.S. Customs rulings): Zonos got 45/103 right (about 44%). Avalara got 82/103 (about 80%), but that figure is for "manual processes assisted by AI," not pure automation. The paper notes there is no accepted standard benchmark. — [arXiv 2412.14179](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.14179)
- **Zonos** says its rebuilt Classify model makes 29.4% fewer errors than its predecessor (two weeks of live traffic) and 33% fewer errors than an unnamed competitor on 1,000 shipments. It gives no absolute error rate. Its docs claim ">90% accuracy out of the box," while its AI page says accuracy varies with input quality. CBP has selected Zonos to modernise tariff classification and duty collection. — [Zonos blog](https://zonos.com/blog/classify-rebuilt); [Zonos docs](https://zonos.com/docs/supply-chain/classify); [TechBuzz](https://www.techbuzznews.com/zonos-selected-by-cbp-to-modernize-tariff-classification-and-duty-collection/)
- **HSCodeComp**: a 632-entry, expert-annotated 10-digit e-commerce benchmark for deep-search agents. A follow-up paper proposes a *deterministic* agentic workflow with interpretable rule reasoning following the HS General Rules of Interpretation. It argues end-to-end LLM prompting fails here and that a non-trivial share of the benchmark's ground-truth labels may violate the GRIs. — [arXiv 2510.19631](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.19631); [arXiv 2605.14857](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.14857)
- A consensus-based multi-agent LLM framework was tested on 3,300 expert-labelled Canadian HTS records. The authors note exact 10-digit classification remains difficult. — [arXiv 2606.16987](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.16987v1)
- TariffTel, a commercial and therefore interested source, tested AI tools: the correct code appeared outright in 34% of cases, a shortlist needing expert verification in 60%, and a hallucinated code in 6%. — [TariffTel](https://www.tarifftel.com/blog/can-ai-give-you-an-accurate-tariff-code/)
- An approximately 89% 10-digit figure attributed to Tarifflo comes from a non-peer-reviewed ResearchGate PDF. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/398232354_AI-Powered_HS_Code_Classification_for_Cross-Border_Trade)
- **Gaia Dynamics** ($7M seed) claims the "first AI application to ace three consecutive U.S. Customs Broker License Examinations." It publishes no classification error rate. — [Gaia Dynamics classification](https://www.gaiadynamics.ai/product/classification); [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/gaia-dynamics-raises-7-million-seed-round-led-by-corazon-capital/)
- **Altana**: it acquired Cervo AI on 21 Jul 2026. Cervo automates writing customs entries, including PGA filings, from unstructured transactional data, and Altana calls the combination "the only fully-agentic solution for customs brokerage." The deal may exceed $100M with earn-outs. — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260721109771/en/Altana-Acquires-Cervo-AI-to-Deliver-End-to-End-Agentic-AI-Platform-for-Customs-Brokerage); [Sourcing Journal/WWD](https://wwd.com/sourcing-journal/trade/altana-acquires-cervo-ai-customs-compliance-trade-cbp-maersk-supply-chain-1239082469/)
- Altana markets Trade Compliance Workflows with an AI agent acting as a "continuous compliance officer 24/7." It claims 8x faster classification plus automated origin determination, duty calculation and tariff stacking (self-reported). — [Altana](https://altana.ai/resources/modern-trade-enforcement)
- In Oct 2025 CBP selected Altana's AI-powered Product Passports. Axios reported the pilot uses "agentic AI systems trained on the Altana product network" to flag issues such as incorrect tariff codes. Reports of the number of CBP agents using Altana conflict (2,000+ vs 3,000+). — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/10/31/cbp-altana-ai-trade); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251001718752/en/U.S.-Customs-and-Border-Protection-Selects-Altanas-AI-Powered-Product-Passports-to-Drive-Next-Generation-Supply-Chain-Traceability-Trusted-Trade)

### Inferences
- For carbonTraceAI, CN-code classification is the gate to CBAM scope: is a product in Annex I or not? Given 6-digit accuracy of roughly 57% at best for open models, a CN-classification agent must be shortlist plus evidence plus human confirm, never auto-commit. The CBAM goods list is short (iron/steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen, electricity), so a constrained "in scope / which CN" classifier over a narrow label set should perform far better than open-ended HS classification. That is an inference and should be tested.
- The academic trend toward deterministic, rule-traced agentic workflows (GRI reasoning steps logged) mirrors the auditability requirement in CBAM.

### Gaps
- No accuracy data was found for Descartes, Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE, Hurricane Commerce or Flexport AI. EU CN 8-digit benchmarks were not found; all the benchmarks cover the U.S. or Canadian schedules.

## 3. Supplier-engagement agents (chasing data, parsing replies)

### Takeaway
I found no shipped ESG product where an autonomous agent emails suppliers, chases them and parses free-text replies or attachments. ESG vendors instead use structured in-platform supplier portals and surveys, with AI assisting the supplier (chatbots) or mapping the returned data. Procurement-side tools (Pactum, Fairmarkit, Zip, Tealbook) were not researched.

### Cited Findings
- Assent uses an AI chatbot for suppliers responding to CBAM data requests, backed by human support. — [Assent](https://www.assent.com/solutions/esg-supply-chain/cbam/)
- A Sweep comparison page says Sweep replaces email-based supplier collection with structured in-platform workflows. Sweepy does "supplier performance analysis." — [Sweep vs Normative](https://www.sweep.net/normative-vs-sweep); [EIN Presswire](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/943872581/sweep-takes-ai-beyond-assistance-to-run-end-to-end-sustainability-workflows)
- Normative runs supplier engagement through structured surveys and uses AI for activity-to-factor matching. It states that many suppliers, especially SMEs, have no emissions data and that AI cannot fill that gap, so proactive engagement is still needed. — [Normative insight](https://normative.io/insight/using-ai-to-tackle-scope-3-emissions/); [Normative supply chain engagement](https://normative.io/platform/supply-chain-engagement/)
- Supplier data is widely described as the hardest part of CBAM: importers need installation-level data from non-EU suppliers unfamiliar with EU formats. — [Coolset](https://www.coolset.com/academy/top-cbam-software-tools-7f11d); [Breathe ESG](https://www.breatheesg.com/resources/cbam-reporting-software)
- Microsoft's ESG value chain solution (2024 preview) collects partner-specific emissions from suppliers. — [Microsoft News](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/2024/02/20/new-data-and-ai-solutions-in-microsoft-cloud-for-sustainability-help-move-organizations-from-pledges-to-progress/)

### Inferences
- The CBAM supplier problem is inverted for carbonTraceAI. Its customers are the suppliers, the non-EU operators receiving requests from EU importers. An agent that ingests importer request emails or templates, maps them to the customer's ERP data and drafts the EU communication template, with human sign-off before sending, appears unaddressed by any product found. Likewise for upstream precursor-supplier chasing, for example a steel producer requesting pig-iron or electricity data.
- Sending messages on a customer's behalf needs explicit approval gates; no vendor found automates outbound sending.

### Gaps
- Pactum, Fairmarkit, Zip, Tealbook, Manufacture 2030, CarbonChain and EcoVadis agent capabilities were not retrieved. I found no evidence of reply-parsing accuracy.

## 4. Document-extraction agents (utility bills, invoices, EPDs)

### Takeaway
Document extraction has shipped widely, and the universal design is extraction plus confidence flagging plus a human exception queue. Vendors claim 90-99%+ accuracy without published test sets. ServiceNow's "highlight AI-extracted fields, require justification on override" is a concrete, auditable UI pattern.

### Cited Findings
- Watershed's PDF scanner processes utility bills, invoices and supplier documents, with a reported 90% reduction in OCR data-entry time (vendor). Watershed itself warns that vendors claiming "99%+ accuracy without caveats are overstating." — [Watershed](https://watershed.com/blog/sustainability-ai-in-2026-what-works-what-doesnt-and-why-the-difference-matters)
- In Arcadia's tariff matching, high-confidence matches flow through automatically and uncertain ones are flagged for expert review. A competitor's page says Urjanet was absorbed into the Arcadia platform; this is unverified. — [Arcadia blog](https://marketing-site-main.us-east-1.prod.arcadia.com/blog/energy-cost-accuracy-scale); [Nectar comparison](https://nectarclimate.com/compare/arcadia-vs-urjanet-vs-energycap)
- Nectar claims 99.2% AI parsing accuracy with human QA on flagged bills. This is self-reported with no methodology. — [Nectar](https://nectarclimate.com/compare/arcadia-vs-urjanet-vs-energycap)
- ServiceNow's sustainability document intelligence extracts consumption, billing dates, amounts and units, and highlights AI-extracted fields for review. Overrides require a mandatory justification. Its docs state results are "as is" and not guaranteed accurate. — [ServiceNow docs](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/s82Zw_idTcTEahshu77BGw/qb7nfXduz_10xuazJfw~xA)

### Inferences
- For CBAM, the high-value documents are electricity bills (indirect emissions), fuel invoices, precursor supplier certificates and lab analyses (carbon content). ServiceNow's flag-and-justify pattern maps directly onto an audit trail that a CBAM verifier could inspect.

### Gaps
- No independent accuracy benchmark for utility-bill or EPD extraction was found. EPD-parsing products were not researched.

## 6. Failures, hallucination risk, auditor/verifier acceptance, and the standards' position on AI

### Takeaway
No assurance or CBAM verification standard addresses AI explicitly. ISSA 5000, effective for periods beginning on or after 15 Dec 2026, is technology-neutral and puts the burden on understanding the reporting process and internal controls. The CBAM verification regulation is likewise silent on AI and requires a physical site visit in an installation's first verification year. Acceptance therefore depends on traceability and controls, not on the AI. A widely cited failure mode is general-purpose LLMs producing untraceable numbers and multi-agent pipelines breaking calculation lineage.

### Cited Findings
- Watershed lists failure modes. General-purpose LLMs produce numbers without traceable sources that auditors can't verify, and "cannot distinguish a defensible estimate from a hallucinated one." Multi-agent workflows without logging break lineage, so auditors "cannot follow the chain." Sensitive supplier data gets uploaded to general cloud AI. In-house agents are costly to keep audit-ready. Watershed claims all audited platform measurements "have passed" under its guaranteed assurance program, with no supporting data. — [Watershed](https://watershed.com/blog/sustainability-ai-in-2026-what-works-what-doesnt-and-why-the-difference-matters)
- LLM non-determinism is acknowledged as a repeatability risk; Watershed reports near-100% repeatability only where one unambiguous factor exists. — [Watershed benchmark](https://watershed.com/blog/how-we-benchmark-and-measure-the-accuracy-of-watershed-ai-for-emissions-factor-mapping)
- HS classification hallucination: 6% of test cases produced entirely hallucinated codes (TariffTel, interested source). Benchmark labels themselves may be wrong (HSCodeComp reanalysis). — [TariffTel](https://www.tarifftel.com/blog/can-ai-give-you-an-accurate-tariff-code/); [arXiv 2605.14857](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.14857)
- ISSA 5000 is effective for assurance engagements on periods beginning on or after 15 Dec 2026, with early application permitted. It requires practitioners to understand the information, criteria, reporting process and internal controls in order to identify risks of material misstatement. — [Protiviti](https://www.protiviti.com/us-en/blogs/issa-5000-sustainability-reporting); [TXCPA](https://www.tx.cpa/news-publications/todays-cpa-magazine/issues/article/november-december-2025/2025/11/05/iaasb-approves-new-standard-on-sustainability-assurance)
- An academic framework (MDPI 2025) proposes "graded reliance ceilings" and non-delegable decisions. It permits clerical assistance, analytical recommendation and constrained agentic execution, but prohibits autonomous AI decisions on materiality, evidence sufficiency and conclusions. It stresses that ISSA 5000 prescribes no AI tools and that no software can be "ISSA 5000 compliant." This is a proposal, not regulation. — [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/3042-6618/2/3/15)
- One guide says Omnibus I removed CSRD's planned move to reasonable assurance (limited assurance remains). This should be checked against the directive text. — [Coolset ISSA 5000 guide](https://www.coolset.com/academy/issa-5000-csrd-guide)
- CBAM verification is governed by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2546 (verification principles and report format), Delegated Regulation 2025/2551 (accreditation) and Implementing Regulation 2025/2547 (calculation methodology). A physical site visit is mandatory in an installation's first verification year. A virtual visit is possible only after a physical visit in the preceding period, and a waiver only after two. — [CBAM Guide verification](https://cbamguide.com/compliance/verification/); [CBAM Guide site visit](https://cbamguide.com/news/2026-08-24-cbam-verification-site-visit-satellite-imagery/)

### Inferences
- Verifiers check a monitoring methodology, data flows and controls at the installation. AI-proposed values are acceptable only if the record shows source document, transformation, rule or factor applied, who approved and when. carbonTraceAI's existing audit trail is the asset, and every agent action should write into it as a "proposal → approval" event with model/version and confidence.
