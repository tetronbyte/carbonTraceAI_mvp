# Compliance carbon markets and carbon taxes worldwide (excluding EU CBAM) as opportunities for an agentic-AI carbon software startup — status as of 21 October 2026

Scope note: This file goes deeper on compliance regimes than the two prior reports (`reports/CBAM agentic workflow market research.md`, `reports/Agentic AI sustainability opportunities.md`). Those reports already established: EU CBAM mechanics; UK CBAM start 1 Jan 2027 with £50k threshold; SA carbon tax R308/t in 2026 rising to R462 by 2030; Türkiye 2026 MRV-only for ≥50 kt CBAM-sector installations; India 490 obligated entities; local competitors Sustrack/Karbonet (Türkiye), CarbonSettle/CleanCarbon.ai/The Sustainability Cloud (India), virESG (Morocco). ## India CCTS: compliance mechanism, first cycle, obligated entities, verification, trading launch, PAT transition

### Takeaway
India's CCTS is live as an intensity-based baseline-and-credit compliance scheme (~490 entities in 7 notified sectors, ~255 iron & steel units pending in a draft), with the first verified filings (Form A) due 31 July 2026, but no Carbon Credit Certificate (CCC) had traded as of early/mid-October 2026 and CERC price floor/ceiling were not published; verifier capacity is thin and mostly provisional (18 ACVAs, 15 provisional). It is the largest new "one-plant-many-regimes" target overlapping CBAM sectors (steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser), but has a crowded low-price domestic services market.

### Cited Findings
**Status / timeline**
- CCTS notified June 2023 under the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act 2022; first GHG Emission Intensity (GEI) targets notified Oct 2025 (aluminium, cement, chlor-alkali, pulp & paper); Jan 2026 (petroleum refining, petrochemicals, textiles); baseline FY2023-24; compliance years FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 (tighter in second year) — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/compliance-obligations-under-indias-carbon-credit-trading-scheme-enter-force-seven-sectors); [crbn.credit](https://crbn.credit/post/india-carbon-credit-certificate-trading-october-2026) (secondary).
- Notifications: G.S.R. 739(E) of 8 Oct 2025 (282 entities) and G.S.R. 25(E) of 13 Jan 2026 (208 entities) = ~490 — secondary summaries via [search of LKS/Carboned/Climes](https://sustainability.lkslaw.com/insights/gei-targets-iron-steel); 282-entity figure also in [UCR Medium](https://medium.com/@UniversalCarbonRegistry/bee-notifies-carbon-intensity-targets-for-282-obligated-entities-under-compliance-market-1b248eb60ce1). Some sources cite ~740 entities across nine sectors (likely including steel/fertiliser drafts) — [CCTS search results, e.g. Orennow](https://www.orennow.com/blog/carbon-credit-trading-scheme-ccts-india); treat as unconfirmed.
- Iron & steel: MoEFCC draft (revised) notification of 26 June 2026 would set plant-level intensity targets for ~255 units (incl. JSW, Tata Steel, SAIL, AM/NS, plus sponge iron/secondary producers), 60-day comment window; not yet binding as of latest sources — [LKS](https://sustainability.lkslaw.com/insights/gei-targets-iron-steel); [Carboned](https://carboned.in/iron-steel-ccts-notification-guide); [Testneeti, 6 Jul 2026](https://testneeti.in/current-affairs/2026-07-06/centre-proposes-mandatory-carbon-emission-targets-for-iron-and-st-jul-2026). Fertiliser: sources conflict on whether targets are notified; weight of detailed sources says "not yet" — [Carbonneeti](https://carbonneeti.com/blog/ccts-india-2025-2026-complete-guide) vs others.
- Indian Carbon Market (ICM) Portal launched 21 Mar 2026 by Power Minister Manohar Lal Khattar; covers registration to CCC issuance, plus validation/verification/accreditation of MRV bodies — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/compliance-obligations-under-indias-carbon-credit-trading-scheme-enter-force-seven-sectors); [Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/digital-pathways-to-net-zero-union-power-minister-launches-indian-carbon-market-portal).
- First compliance filing (Form A, verified FY2025-26 data) due 31 July 2026 — [crbn.credit](https://crbn.credit/post/india-carbon-credit-certificate-trading-october-2026); [Reclimatize](https://reclimatize.in/ccts-enforcement-commercial-rational/) (secondary). Missing baseline data → deemed baseline at sector average (per Reclimatize, unverified against rules).

**Trading launch**
- CERC (Terms and Conditions for Purchase and Sale of Carbon Credit Certificates) Regulations 2026 notified Feb 2026 (gazetted ~April 2026; sources differ on exact date); trading on IEX, PXIL, HPX in monthly sessions, no OTC at launch; two segments (compliance, offset); floor/ceiling to be set by CERC before trading — [crbn.credit](https://crbn.credit/post/india-carbon-credit-certificate-trading-october-2026).
- As of ~7 Oct 2026, no CCC had traded and no floor/ceiling had been published; expectations range from October to November 2026 (issuance Oct, trading Nov per one source) — [Reclimatize price formation](https://reclimatize.in/india-ccc-carbon-credit-market-iex-trading-price-formation/); [carboncreditconsulting.in](https://carboncreditconsulting.in/blog/ccts-guide-india-2026); [SaurEnergy](https://www.saurenergy.com/solar-energy-news/indias-carbon-market-edges-toward-its-first-trade-12482615). Earlier official guidance said "mid-2026" — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/compliance-obligations-under-indias-carbon-credit-trading-scheme-enter-force-seven-sectors). Slippage is clear.
- Price: no market price; analyst scenarios Rs 400–900/t (≈ USD 4.5–10) or Rs 250–1,500/t; a pilot-registry trade near Rs 1,740 described as illiquid/not a reference — [Reclimatize](https://reclimatize.in/india-ccc-carbon-credit-market-iex-trading-price-formation/); [crbn.credit](https://crbn.credit/post/india-carbon-credit-certificate-trading-october-2026). All estimates, not forecasts.

**MRV / verification**
- BEE's official ACVA register (accessed Oct 2026) lists **18 Accredited Carbon Verification Agencies**: 15 provisional, 3 final; 12 compliance-only, 5 offset-only, 1 both (Bureau Veritas India, the only final-accredited compliance verifier, covering cement, iron & steel, petrochemical, refinery). Others include PGS Energy Services, Encon, FICCI, CII, NIN Energy, Namdhari, East Coast Sustainable (status "Hold"), TÜV India (offset), Earthood (offset), KBS (offset), URS (offset); latest three added 7 Oct 2026. In March 2026 the list had only 2 entries — [BEE ACVA list](https://beeindia.gov.in/view_content.php?lid=568&lang=1); [BEE PDF Mar 2026](https://beeindia.gov.in/sites/default/files/list_ccts_agency.pdf).
- Process (secondary): baseline → ICM registration → ACVA verification (~8–12 weeks) → Form A → BEE 10-day completeness check + >30-day technical review → CCC issuance/shortfall calculation → exchange trade → surrender — [Reclimatize](https://reclimatize.in/ccts-enforcement-commercial-rational/). Reports submitted after third-party verification within four months of compliance-year end — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/india-to-launch-carbon-market-trading-within-four-months/).

**Penalty**
- Environmental compensation = 2× average CCC price in the trading cycle per tonne of shortfall, levied by CPCB, payable within 90 days; no instalments/waivers; banking unlimited, borrowing prohibited — [Reclimatize](https://reclimatize.in/ccts-enforcement-commercial-rational/) (secondary; check against GEI Target Rules 2025 / EP Act). Because no CCC has traded, the penalty amount is currently undefined.

**PAT transition**
- Government statement in Parliament: nine GHG-intensive PAT sectors (incl. refineries, iron & steel, textiles) transition gradually to CCTS by FY2026-27; four remaining energy-intensive sectors incl. thermal power stay under PAT — [ORF](https://www.orfonline.org/research/a-better-blueprint-uncertain-outcomes-assessing-india-s-carbon-trading-scheme) / [Practicemock summary](https://www.practicemock.com/blog/vishleshan-for-regulatory-exams-check-daily-news-analysis-19th-june-2025/) (secondary). Sources conflict on which PAT cycle (VII vs VIII) closes in 2026.
- PAT compliance track record poor: non-compliance rose from ~9% of Designated Consumers in Cycle I to ~56% in Cycle II; DCs obligated to buy 86% of mandated ESCerts found unregistered — [Sattva/ORF via search](https://www.sattva.co.in/quick-read/is-indias-carbon-market-ready-for-what-is-coming/).

**Offsets**
- Separate CCTS offset mechanism for non-obligated entities (offset segment on exchanges); Article 6 project registration through ICM portal — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/compliance-obligations-under-indias-carbon-credit-trading-scheme-enter-force-seven-sectors); [crbn.credit](https://crbn.credit/post/india-carbon-credit-certificate-trading-october-2026). Whether obligated entities can surrender offset-mechanism CCCs for compliance, and any limit, was not confirmed.

**Software / services vendors**
- Vendor pages marketing CCTS compliance tools: The Sustainability Cloud ([CCTS software](https://www.thesustainabilitycloud.com/ccts-india-compliance-software/)), Climes ([CCTS reporting](https://www.climes.io/ccts-reporting-india)), Breathe ESG ([CCTS explainer](https://www.breatheesg.com/resources/what-is-ccts)), Oren ([blog](https://www.orennow.com/blog/carbon-credit-trading-scheme-ccts-india)), Circulogy services ([CCTS services](https://circulogy.com/ccts-compliance-services/)), Carboned playbooks ([Carboned](https://carboned.in/ccts-compliance-playbook-obligated-entities)). Prior report: Indian MSME CBAM consultant price anchor ~Rs 2 lakh/yr.

### Inferences
- Verifier bottleneck mirrors CBAM: 13 compliance-capable ACVAs (mostly provisional, 1-year validity) for ~490 entities (→ ~745 if steel added) implies ~35–60 entities per verifier in the first cycle; evidence packs that shorten ACVA review are directly valuable.
- Agent-shaped tasks: (1) extract FY2023-24 baseline and FY2025-26 activity data from energy bills/PAT Form-1 style records and DCS logs; (2) map PAT "specific energy consumption" data to CCTS GEI boundaries (reuse PAT data); (3) draft ACVA evidence packs and respond to BEE technical-review queries; (4) CCC position/strategy (surplus/shortfall vs 2× penalty, bank vs sell); (5) reconcile CCTS GEI with CBAM embedded-emissions for steel/aluminium exporters (India is the largest CBAM-exposed emerging exporter besides China/Türkiye).
- Clear payer (obligated entity) exists, but price per tonne is low and uncertain; value comes from avoiding 2× penalty and selling surplus. Large incumbents (Tata, JSW, Hindalco) likely in-house/Big-4; mid-size textile, chlor-alkali, pulp & paper, secondary steel are the weak-tooling segment.
- Domestic price competition (Indian consultancies at ~$2k/yr) makes India a poor first market for an Africa-based team; better as later expansion on the same engine.

### Gaps
- No confirmed first CCC trade date or CERC floor/ceiling (as of sources up to ~7 Oct 2026).
- No official per-sector entity counts; unresolved 490 vs ~740 discrepancy; fertiliser target status.
- Whether Form A filings were actually completed on 31 July 2026 or extended — not found.
- Offset-to-compliance fungibility and limits not confirmed.
- No pricing data for CCTS software tools.

## China national ETS: expansion to steel, cement, aluminium; MRV burden; domestic software players

### Takeaway
China's ETS added ~1,500 steel/cement/aluminium entities in March 2025 (total ~3,300–3,700 entities, ~8 Gt CO2), with a 2024–2026 "familiarisation" phase (free allocation = verified emissions for 2024; sector-balanced intensity allocation 2025–26) and tightening from 2027; MRV burden is the heaviest of any regime (monthly evidence-backed reporting within 40 days, 270+ automated platform checks, unannounced inspections, large fraud penalties), but the market is closed to foreign software practically and served by domestic players and the state platform — not a realistic market for carbonTraceAI.

### Cited Findings
- MEE expansion (March 2025): ~1,500 new companies in cement, steel, aluminium; ~3 Gt CO2e added; total ~3,700 entities and ~8 Gt (~15% of global emissions); scope = energy and process CO2, plus CF4/C2F6 for aluminium; indirect excluded — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/china-officially-expands-national-ets-cement-steel-and-aluminum-sectors); [Xinhua (CN)](https://www.news.cn/energy/20250327/bbd3996d998f490ba6410319e396068b/c.html).
- Counts conflict: one source gives official 2025 count 3,378 (2,087 power, 232 steel, 962 cement, 97 aluminium) — [The Planet Brief](https://theplanetbrief.com/carbon/china-national-ets-explained/) (secondary); IEEFA cites 1,334 additional entities — [IEEFA](https://ieefa.org/resources/chinas-emissions-trading-system-ets-reforms-track-needs-robust-enforcement).
- Allocation: 2024 free allowances = verified 2024 emissions; first compliance deadline end-2025 for 2024 emissions; 2025–2026 output-based intensity allocation balanced within each sector; Phase 2 from 2027 tightens with advanced benchmarks; no absolute cap planned yet per ICAP, though Chinese reports say a "clear, transparent sector cap" is planned after 2027 — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/china-officially-expands-national-ets-cement-steel-and-aluminum-sectors); [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/energy/20250327/bbd3996d998f490ba6410319e396068b/c.html).
- MRV: monthly emission reports with supporting evidence within 40 calendar days after month-end (fuel use, calorific values, carbon content, electricity, output); three-tier review (national/provincial/municipal); defaults allowed where measurement infeasible; MEE exploring CEMS — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/china-officially-expands-national-ets-cement-steel-and-aluminum-sectors); [ESGOS](https://esgos.org/en/intel/cn-ets-explainer) (secondary). Feb 2026 notice requires data quality control plans via the national platform and monthly "information-based evidence deposit" within 40 days; non-listed steel/cement firms ≥26 kt CO2e must also report annually — [search summary of MEE notice / stdaily](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-02/11/content_473401.html).
- National Carbon Market Management Platform (launched Feb 2023) has 270+ intelligent checking rules and uses AI for anomaly identification; 2025 progress report describes five unannounced inspections — [MEE 2025 progress report](https://xcoss.henan.gov.cn/typtfile/20251009/1613827be7d04b2aaa0cfc06b84f458e.pdf); [MEE 2024 report](https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywdt/xwfb/202407/W020240722528850763859.pdf).
- Penalties for falsification: RMB 0.5–2m fines or 5–10× illegal gains, 50–100% cut in next year's allowances on refusal to correct; consultants/verifiers committing data fraud face up to 10× penalties — [ESGOS](https://esgos.org/en/intel/cn-ets-explainer); [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/ets/china-national-ets).
- Upcoming deadlines: 2027 covered lists by 31 Oct 2026; 2027 data quality control plans by 31 Dec 2026 — [ESGOS](https://esgos.org/en/intel/cn-ets-explainer) (secondary).
- Price: CEA closed RMB 97.56/t on 16 Oct 2026 (≈ USD 13.7); Jan–Aug 2026 average RMB 83.86/t (+14% vs 2025 avg ~RMB 73); CCER offset cap 5% of verified emissions; CCER relaunched 22 Jan 2024 — [CarbonMeld](https://carbonmeld.com/en/articles/china-ets-cea-breakout-ccer-consultations/); [Carbon Pulse](https://carbon-pulse.com/474771/) (secondary aggregation).
- Software: Carbonstop described as China's leading carbon-accounting software provider with 1,000+ corporate users (Alibaba, Tencent) — [Global Growth Insights](https://www.globalgrowthinsights.com/blog/carbon-management-software-companies-796) (low-quality market-research blog).

### Inferences
- The MRV burden (monthly evidence deposits, CEMS, AI-screened platform, criminal-scale fraud penalties) is exactly the kind of recurring evidence work agents can help with, but the state platform already does AI anomaly checks, data-localisation and political factors exclude a foreign entrant. Use China as a design reference (monthly evidence cadence, automated rule checks), not a market.
- Relevance for carbonTraceAI: Chinese CBAM exporters will compete with African producers; China's ETS price (~$14) counts as "carbon price paid" under CBAM, which raises the bar for African producers' deduction arguments.

### Gaps
- Domestic software vendor landscape (e.g., state-owned group platforms, Envision, Sinocarbon) not found in reliable sources.
- No primary MEE figure for the exact 2025/2026 entity count by sector.
- Third-party verification arrangements (government-procured vs. firm-paid) not confirmed for new sectors.

## East and Southeast Asia: Japan GX-ETS, Korea K-ETS Phase 4, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore

### Takeaway
Japan's GX-ETS (mandatory from 1 Apr 2026, ~300–400 companies ≥100 kt; first verified emissions report 30 Sep 2027) and Vietnam's pilot (110 facilities, exchange live since 29 Jun 2026) are the two genuinely new compliance cohorts in 2026; Korea is mature (813 entities) with strong local vendors; Indonesia's ETS has barely traded; Thailand and Malaysia have slipped (no enacted law/gazetted tax); Singapore is small (tens of facilities) but has the clearest offset-linkage (5% Article 6 credits). None is a natural fit for an Africa-based team except as later expansion.

### Cited Findings
**Japan GX-ETS**
- Mandatory from 1 Apr 2026 for operators with average direct CO2 ≥100,000 t over FY2023–FY2025; estimated 300–400 companies, ~60% of Japan's emissions — [GlobalDeal](https://globaldeal.io/blog/japans-gx-ets-the-meetings-changed); [Offset8](https://offset8capital.com/articles/japan-carbon-credit-market-gx-ets-guide/). One source says ~600 companies — [Asset Impact/GRESB](https://asset-impact.gresb.com/news-and-impact/japans-carbon-market-ets). Conflict unresolved.
- FY2026 price corridor: ceiling ¥4,300/t (≈USD 28), floor ¥1,700 (≈USD 11); same for FY2027; shortfall charge = shortfall × ceiling × 1.1; offsets (J-Credits, JCM) up to ~10% of emissions; allowance trading not expected until autumn 2027 — [GlobalDeal](https://globaldeal.io/blog/japans-gx-ets-the-meetings-changed); [Tech4Impact](https://tech4impactsummit.com/blog/japan-gx-ets-mandatory-2026/); [Offset8](https://offset8capital.com/articles/japan-carbon-credit-market-gx-ets-guide/).
- Timeline: by 30 Sep 2026 submit average-emissions notification and transition plan via ERMS (G-Biz ID); 30 Sep 2027 file allocation info and verified FY2026 emissions; end-Nov 2027 allocation; 31 Jan 2028 holding deadline — [LPA Gyosei](https://www.lpa-gyosei.com/post/japan-mandatory-gx-ets-fy2026). ICAP page cites a 31 Jan reporting deadline (likely pre-mandatory) — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/ets/japan-gx-ets).
- Verification: METI-registered verification bodies (5-year renewal); limited assurance FY2026–FY2028, reasonable assurance from FY2029; METI certified seven verifiers by mid-June 2026 (26 agencies had served the voluntary GX League phase but need separate certification) — [QCI, 17 Jun 2026](https://www.qcintel.com/carbon/article/japan-certifies-seven-emission-auditors-for-gx-ets-67052.html) (paywalled; partial); [search summary](https://environment-and-law-kyoto.jp/en/2026/06/17/01-gx-ets/).
- Vendors: Asuene (founded 2019, ~10,000 clients claimed, acquired US nZero) — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/asuene-enters-u-s-climate-tech-market-with-acquisition-of-carbon-accounting-startup-nzero/); Asuene markets GX-ETS data-integrity content — [Asuene](https://asuene.com/us/blog/why-data-integrity-will-define-success-under-japans-gx-ets); Zeroboard (GHG calculation cloud, ISO 14064-3-verified outputs claimed) — [Zeroboard](https://en.zeroboard.jp/service/zeroboard).

**Korea K-ETS Phase 4 (2026–2030)**
- 813 covered entities (2025), ~77.75% of national emissions; Phase 4 cap 2,537.3 Mt incl. reserves; power auction share 15% (2026) → 50% (2030); other non-EITE sectors 15% by 2030; EITE (steel, non-ferrous, petrochem, cement, semiconductors) 100% free; 260m auctioned allowances (~11% of cap); 85m to new quantity-based K-MSR — [ICAP K-ETS](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/ets/korea-emissions-trading-system-k-ets); [ICAP Phase 4](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/korea-approves-phase-4-k-ets-allocation-plan-2026-2030).
- Prices 2025: average auction KRW 9,956 (USD 6.99), secondary KRW 9,393 (USD 6.60); 2026 reports of KRW 12,700 auction clearing and claims of >KRW 20,000 are unverified — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/ets/korea-emissions-trading-system-k-ets); [green.earth](https://www.green.earth/news/south-koreas-carbon-market-tightens-as-blue-carbon-trading-moves-closer).
- Offsets: domestic + international (Article 6) capped at 5% of obligation; in 2024 1.9m KOCs converted, only 71,491 submitted for compliance; MRV: annual report by end-March, third-party verification, certification by end-May; penalty up to 3× average market price — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/ets/korea-emissions-trading-system-k-ets).

**Indonesia**
- Presidential Regulation 110/2025 (10 Oct 2025) replaces PR 98/2021 — [vLex](https://vlex.com/vid/indonesia-introduces-new-carbon-1105015271). Phase 1 covered 99 PLN-connected coal units (~37% of capacity); Phase 2 (2025–2027) adds captive coal >25 MW and gas plants; World Bank dashboard: 563 installations (up from 146) — [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/ets/indonesian-economic-value-carbon-nilai-ekonomi-karbon-trading-scheme); [World Bank dashboard](https://carbonpricingdashboard.worldbank.org/compliance-factsheet/ETS_ID).
- IDXCarbon cumulative volume ~1.98 Mt by April 2026 at ~IDR 47k/t implied; no PTBAE-PU (allowances) traded on IDXCarbon; demand resembles voluntary market — [ResearchGate paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/414719584_Emission_Integrity_and_the_Readiness_of_IDX-Listed_Issuers_Optimizing_the_Carbon_Exchange_to_Support_Indonesia's_Net-Zero_Emission_Target) (single academic source). IEEFA: caps too lenient, little compliance demand — [IEEFA](https://ieefa.org/resources/two-years-after-launch-indonesias-carbon-market-struggles-find-momentum).
- IDR 30,000/t carbon tax repeatedly delayed, not in force (March 2026); plan to add nine industries to ETS from 2027 — [IISD policy brief Mar 2026](https://www.iisd.org/system/files/2026-03/carbon-pricing-southeast-asia.pdf) (via search summary; PDF not machine-readable).

**Vietnam**
- Decree 119/2025 (Aug 2025) sets ETS design; Decision 263/QĐ-TTg (9 Feb 2026) caps 243.1 Mt (2025) and 268.4 Mt (2026) for 34 thermal power, 25 steel, 51 cement plants; Decision 699/QĐ-BNNMT (27 Feb 2026) allocates to 110 facilities; ~40% of national emissions; free allocation in pilot, auctions from 2029; offsets up to 30% of liable emissions; borrowing ≤15% — [VnEconomy](https://en.vneconomy.vn/pilot-2026-ghg-emission-quotas-to-be-allocated.htm); [Baochinhphu](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/pilot-greenhouse-gas-emission-quotas-for-20252026-period-approved-111260210153854027.htm); [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/energy-transition/061125-vietnam-to-start-pilot-emission-trading-in-2025-allow-offsetting-30-emissions).
- Exchange: Decree 29/2026 (19 Jan 2026); carbon exchange opened 29 Jun 2026 on Hanoi Stock Exchange with VSDC settlement; >1,200 t traded on day one; six securities firms in pilot — [The Investor](https://theinvestor.vn/vietnam-officially-launches-first-carbon-trading-exchange-d19425.html); [ETP](https://www.energytransitionpartnership.org/2026/06/30/vietnam-officializes-domestic-carbon-market-exchange/).

**Thailand**
- Climate Change Bill approved by Cabinet Dec 2025, sent to Council of State; enactment expected ~Q3 2027; draft carbon tax on 31 goods categories via Excise/Customs; ETS needs further approval; proposal allows credits up to 15%; fuel excise restructured at THB 200/tCO2 from 2025 — [Nishimura & Asahi](https://www.nishimura.com/en/knowledge/publications/20260115-118561); [Reccessary](https://www.reccessary.com/en/news/thailand-climate-change-bill); [Serrari](https://serrarigroup.com/thailand-climate-bill-targets-carbon-pricing-and-finance-by-2027/).

**Malaysia**
- Carbon tax on iron & steel and energy announced for 2026 (Budget 2025/2026), but as of July 2026 on hold — no rate or start date gazetted; NRES Minister (Apr 2026) said timeline "being reviewed in light of current geopolitical circumstances"; Climate Change Bill not passed; rate rumours RM15/t vs RM35–45/t — [Cobler](https://cobler.io/blog/carbon-tax-malaysia) (secondary); [EY Budget 2026](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-my/technical/take-5-business-alert/documents/ey-take-5-budget-2026.pdf); [S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/energy-transition/102124-malaysia-sets-2026-carbon-tax-reaffirms-decarbonization-goals-in-budget-2025).

**Singapore**

### Inferences
- "Most entities with weakest tooling and a clear payer": India CCTS mid-size entities (textiles, chlor-alkali, pulp & paper, secondary steel) and South African mid-size industrial emitters. India has more entities; SA has the higher price (R308 ≈ $17 vs CCC est. $5–10), the producer as payer, no software incumbent and the strongest fit with carbonTraceAI's Africa positioning and CBAM deduction story.
- Offset-eligibility agent opportunity is real but fragmented: each regime has distinct eligibility rules (Chile domestic certified projects; Colombia non-causation certificates; SA Offset Regulations with 10/15% caps; Singapore NEA list + Article 6 LoA + corresponding adjustment; Japan J-Credit/JCM; Vietnam 30%). A deterministic rules table per regime plus LLM document checks (registry statements, LoAs, methodology eligibility) is the defensible design; avoid tying revenue to credit prices (prior report's VCM warning).
- Generic agent-shaped tasks recurring across all regimes: monitoring-plan drafting (Brazil 2027 A1, Türkiye, Japan ERMS transition plans, EU CNPs); activity-data extraction from invoices/meters; verifier evidence packs (India ACVAs, Japan registered bodies, Türkiye accredited verifiers, UK/EU CBAM); regulator-query response drafting (BEE technical review, METI, DFFE); allowance/credit position strategy (bank/sell/buy vs penalty multiples: India 2×, Japan 1.1× ceiling, Korea ≤3×).
- Verification capacity is the common bottleneck in every new regime (India 18 ACVAs mostly provisional; Japan 7 certified verifiers by June 2026; CBAM zero accredited as of July 2026 per prior report) — verifier-facing evidence packs are the highest-leverage product across regimes.
