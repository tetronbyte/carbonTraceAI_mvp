# Carbon verification, assurance and MRV professional services as a buyer for agentic AI (as of Oct 2026)

Scope: verifiers and VVBs, assurance providers and carbon consultants as buyers of AI agents, instead of SMEs. This is the "Harvey/Basis for carbon" thesis. Research date: 21 Oct 2026. Many sources are vendor or consultancy blogs. These are flagged where used. Prior internal work is in `reports/CBAM agentic workflow market research.md` and `research_notes/CBAM agentic workflow market research/`.

## 1. Verification demand 2026-2028 and verifier shortage evidence

### Takeaway
Demand is rising sharply, and it is regulation-driven. The sources are EU CBAM (first real verification cycle Q4 2026 to Sep 2027), UK CBAM (from Jan 2027), ISSA 5000 (effective Dec 2026), SB 253 limited assurance (from ~2027), India CCTS and continuing EU ETS/CORSIA/VCM work. Supply is thin where the new demand is. As of mid-Oct 2026 there are only about 5 accredited CBAM verifiers, all EU-based, against ~40,000 installations. India has 1-2 ACVAs on the BEE register. Hard figures on verifier headcount, man-days and backlog are scarce.

### Cited Findings
**EU CBAM**
- About 40,000 production installations need verification, and accredited-verifier capacity is insufficient (DIHK position). — [DIHK](https://www.dihk.de/de/newsroom/dihk-impuls-cbam-anpassungen-dringend-noetig-182882)
- As of 13 Oct 2026, 5 CBAM accreditations were documented, while the Commission's official list still showed 0 names (secondary tracker):
  - EmiCert S.A. (Greece/ESYD; scope annex 14 Sep 2026, announced 23 Sep)
  - Bureau Veritas Italia (Accredia; decision 28 Sep 2026)
  - RINA Services (Accredia)
  - SGS ICS Italia (Accredia; partial activity scope)
  - Intertek Certification AB (Swedac, 13 Oct 2026)

  — [cbamguide accredited verifiers](https://cbamguide.com/compliance/accredited-verifiers/)
- Accreditation bodies:
  - 18 national accreditation bodies were ready to accept CBAM applications on 7 Oct 2026, up from 16 on 29 Sep. Only 7 accredit bodies based outside the EU (COFRAC, ESYD, Accredia, RvA, PCA, SNAS, Swedac).
  - RvA cooperates with Türkiye's TURKAK and Swedac with Ukraine's NAAU.
  - EA-6/05 INF (5 Oct 2026) lets the EEA accreditation body outsource assessment to, or use assessors of, the verifier's local accreditation body. The EEA body still issues the accreditation.

  — [cbamguide](https://cbamguide.com/compliance/accredited-verifiers/)
- All documented CBAM verifiers are EU-based. Non-EU installations therefore depend on EU verifiers travelling for the mandatory first-period site visit. — [cbamguide](https://cbamguide.com/compliance/accredited-verifiers/)
- Timeline:
  - Verifier registry registration opened 1 Sep 2026.
  - First in-Registry verification reports are expected Jan 2027.
  - The first CBAM declaration (2026 imports) is due 30 Sep 2027.

  — [cbamguide](https://cbamguide.com/compliance/accredited-verifiers/)
- A physical site visit is required in an installation's first verification year ([EUR-Lex 2025/2546](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32025R2546)). A draft reported by Argus said physical visits should occur at least every two years, with virtual visits only in "serious, extraordinary and unforeseeable circumstances". — [Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2752703-cbam-price-method-verification-drafts-leaked)
- Verification fee estimates (all vendor or secondary, none official):
  - €5k-50k per installation. — [cbamguide](https://cbamguide.com/importers/verification/)
  - €5-15k small, €15-50k medium, €50-150k+ large industrial. — [iFactory](https://ifactoryapp.com/industries/steel-plant/cbam-verification-body-selection-guide-for-steel-exporters)
  - €3-15k. — [The Trade Hub](https://www.thetradehub.eu/en/guides/cbam/verification)
  - INR 5-15 lakh for Indian exporters. — [The Sustainability Cloud](https://www.thesustainabilitycloud.com/blog/eu-cbam-verifiers-complete-guide-and-checklist-for-indian-exporters-2026-ready/)
  - Roughly 25% of a quote is driven by number of sites, travel and days on site (illustrative). — [iFactory](https://ifactoryapp.com/industries/steel-plant/cbam-verification-body-selection-guide-for-steel-exporters)
  - Local verifier networks are claimed to be 40-50% cheaper than flying in EU verifiers. — [virESG](https://viresg.com/en/cbam-morocco)
- Verifier capacity was described as "acute" as of April 2026. No defensible published figure compares accredited capacity with installations needing a first visit. — [cbamguide verification](https://cbamguide.com/importers/verification/)

**UK CBAM**

**China ETS**

**SB 253**

- A "shortage of qualified verifiers" claim appears only in vendor material. — [UL](https://www.ul.com/insights/assurance-gap-are-you-ready-third-party-verification-under-sb-253); [Sustain-CERT](https://www.sustain-cert.com/news/california-sb253-assurance)

**Voluntary carbon market**
- Verra's 2025 VVB Performance Monitoring results ([Verra PMP](https://verra.org/validation-verification/vvb-performance-monitoring-program-pmp/)):
  - 21 VVBs appear.
  - About 462 VCS Energy & Industry, 165 nature-based (NCS) and 90 VCS+CCBS reviews were closed.
  - Average rounds of findings were 1.0-2.6 per VVB.
  - Approval rates were as low as 67% (TÜV Nord, E&I) and 50% (KBS, VCS+CCBS).
  - Average findings per nature-based review reached 30 (RINA).
- Verra acknowledged that demand stretched its capacity and slowed listings, registrations and issuances. It is automating procedures and building a VVB auditing team. — [Verra](https://verra.org/?p=20438); [Verra](https://verra.org/?p=23159)
- Verra suspended 4 VVBs in early 2025, affecting about 21m t of annual supply (headline only; paywalled). — [QCI](https://www.qcintel.com/carbon/article/verra-s-vvb-suspension-impacts-21m-of-annual-supply-data-38870.html)
- A VCS VVB may not verify more than 6 consecutive years of a project. — via [ACR/VCS docs in search](https://verra.org/programs/verified-carbon-standard/)
- UNFCCC lists a "shortage of practitioners with verified competencies" as a GHG verification capacity gap. — [UNFCCC](https://unfccc.int/node/656814)
- A Feb 2026 Perspectives/GIZ/VCMI report on strengthening African VVB capacity exists, but the PDF was too large to fetch. — [VCMI report](https://vcmintegrity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Strengthening-VVB-Capacities-Report_February-2026-v4.pdf)

### Inferences
- Rough CBAM verification revenue pool (my arithmetic, not sourced): if 10-20k of the ~40k installations seek actual-value verification in cycle 1 at a mid-range €10-25k, the fee pool is about €100-500m per year. Most of it goes to a handful of TIC majors in year 1. Many installations will instead fall back on default values.
- The binding constraint in 2026-27 is accredited verifier-days plus travel, not demand. Anything that cuts desk-review days per installation, or makes remote (non-site) work more productive, raises verifier throughput directly. It does not reduce the number of site visits, which regulation fixes. Only about 25% of a quote is travel and site time; the remaining roughly 75% is desk work that software can plausibly compress.
- In China, verifiers are government-designated and government-paid. China is therefore a public-procurement play and is closed to a foreign startup.
- In India, the ACVA pool is tiny (BV India plus provisional bodies). Demand there will run through the same TIC majors and a few local firms (Earthood, VKU, Astraleus). That is a concentrated but reachable buyer set.
- The Verra PMP data shows rework is common: multiple rounds of findings, up to 30 findings per nature-based review. This is quantifiable friction that a pre-review agent (Isometric-style) targets.

### Gaps
- No official count of CBAM-accredited verifiers on the Commission list as of late Oct 2026 (0 official, 5 documented by a tracker).
- No EU-wide count of accredited ETS verifiers or of individual lead verifiers, and no headcount of the GHG verification workforce globally. This could not be sized from public sources.
- No published man-day norms or day rates for EU ETS or CBAM verification were found. Fee data are vendor estimates.
- No Gold Standard or Isometric backlog figures, no current Verra queue times, and no CORSIA verifier count.
- The South African (SANAS) GHG verifier landscape was not found in searches.

## 2. Who the verifiers are and how they work

### Takeaway
The market is dominated by the TIC majors: BV, SGS, TÜV SÜD/Rheinland/Nord, DNV, Intertek, RINA, LRQA, Kiwa. Big-4 firms hold about 55% of corporate sustainability assurance. A long tail of specialist VVBs (Earthood, KBS, 4K Earth Science, EPIC, Aster Global, SCS) dominates VCM work. The workflow is a standardised, document-heavy loop under ISO 14064-3, AVR/CBAM rules and ISAE/ISSA:
1. Contract and time allocation.
2. Strategic analysis and risk analysis.
3. Verification plan and sampling.
4. Site visit.
5. Data re-performance.
6. Findings log and corrections.
7. Independent review.
8. Opinion and report.

### Cited Findings
- All first CBAM accreditations went to TIC majors plus one Greek specialist (EmiCert): BV Italia, RINA, SGS Italia, Intertek Sweden. — [cbamguide](https://cbamguide.com/compliance/accredited-verifiers/)
- Verra's 2025 VVB list includes TÜV Nord, TÜV SÜD South Asia, RINA, Earthood, KBS Certification, 4K Earth Science and LGAI Technological Center. — [Verra PMP](https://verra.org/validation-verification/vvb-performance-monitoring-program-pmp/)
- Audit firms performed 55% of sustainability assurance for large companies in 2023. — [IFAC](https://www.ifac.org/news-events/2025-05/more-global-companies-seek-assurance-sustainability-reporting-study-ifac-aicpa-cima-shows)
- The Commission's 141-page CBAM verification guidance (24 Aug 2026) requires:
  - reasonable assurance at 5% materiality per CN code, where "materiality is not a tolerance band";
  - professional scepticism;
  - treating as inherent risks complex data systems, manual transfers, wrong emission factors and wrong precursors;
  - a limitation of scope (effectively a negative opinion) when primary data are missing or the methodology cannot be followed.

  The guidance never mentions AI. — [Commission guidance (via Eurometal)](https://eurometal.net/wp-content/uploads/Guidance-on-CBAM-verification-and-accreditation-for-verifiers-and-national-accreditation-bodies.pdf), summarised in the prior internal report
- Verifiers access operator data in the CBAM Registry's operator/verifier portal (O3CI). Only one verifier can be active per installation, and "a saved record is not a verified one". — [Materia Intel](https://www.materiaintel.com/insight/cbam_registry_o3ci_operator_verifier_portal)
- VVBs review the project plan and GHG assertion against the methodology to judge whether reductions are "real" and with what confidence. They must be ISO 14065-accredited. — [ACR V&V Standard](https://acrcarbon.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/2023.05.29-ACR-VV-Standard_V1.1_May-31-2018.pdf)
- The FCPF V&V guidelines (v2.8.1, Jan 2026) list the mandatory elements of an opinion (project ID, GHG statement and period, responsible party, and so on). This is a de facto report template. — [FCPF](https://www.forestcarbonpartnership.org/sites/default/files/documents/validation_and_verification_guidelines_v2.8_0.pdf)
- BV was hiring a Lead GHG Verifier (Texas) in July 2026, requiring at least 3 years of GHG verification or sustainability consulting experience. — [BV careers](https://careers.bureauveritas.com/job/Remote-Senior-Sustainability-Consultant-Texa/1372844533/)

### Inferences
- The workflow steps that are document-matching, re-performance and template-filling map well onto agents:
  - strategic and risk analysis drafting from prior-year files;
  - sampling plan generation;
  - tracing figures to invoices, meter logs and lab certificates (DataSnipper-style);
  - re-calculating emissions;
  - drafting the findings log;
  - assembling the report against a mandatory-elements checklist.

  Judgment steps should stay human: materiality decisions, the opinion, and independent review.
- Buyers are concentrated. About 8-10 TIC groups plus the Big-4 control most compliance verification. These are enterprise sales with global IT and security and build-vs-buy instincts.

### Gaps
- No public data on average verification hours split by workflow step. The claim that most hours are desk work is an inference from the about-25% travel/site share.
- Nothing found on ERM CVS, Aster Global, EPIC or African local verifiers' capacity or headcount specifically.

## 3. Existing tooling and AI agents sold to verifiers/assurers

### Takeaway
Financial audit has well-funded AI tooling sold to auditors: DataSnipper (unicorn, 400k+ users, agents launched Oct 2025) and MindBridge. Carbon verification has almost no equivalent. The only clear AI verification agent found is Isometric's in-house Certify, which it uses on its own registry with a small group of verifiers and has published no metrics for. TIC majors are buying or building their own capability. This is white space, but it is also evidence that no one has proven verifiers will pay.

### Cited Findings
**Financial-audit analogues**
- DataSnipper:
  - Raised a $100M Series B at a $1B valuation led by Index Ventures (Feb 2024). — [DataSnipper](https://www.datasnipper.com/resources/datasnipper-raises-100m)

  - Raised $42.3M (investor plus government) in 2017-19. — [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-ottawa-ai-startup-mindbridge-secures-296-million-in-federal-and)

- Operator-side CBAM tools (Sustrack, Karbonet) give verifiers read-only access instead of selling to them. — prior internal note [Sustrack](https://sustrack.ai/products/s-cbam); [Karbonet](https://karbonet.com/cbam-software)
- Searches found no Big-4 product launch of a GHG-assurance AI agent. A 2026 paper studies how the UK Big Four disclose AI use in audit and sustainability assurance. — [JRFM 2026](https://doi.org/10.3390/jrfm19090675)
- Searches for a startup selling an AI copilot specifically to GHG verification bodies or VVBs returned none.

### Inferences
- DataSnipper's crude revenue per user (about $44.5M across 400k+ users, roughly $100/user/yr) is low. Even the best audit-AI analogue monetises per seat inside Excel at modest ARPU, with scale coming from Big-4 firm-wide deals. The carbon verifier population is orders of magnitude smaller (thousands, not hundreds of thousands, by inference). A seat-based model alone will not build a venture-scale business. Pricing must be per engagement or per installation verified.
- The most credible "carbon audit copilot" today sits inside a registry or certifier (Isometric), not with independent VVBs. Registries and standards bodies such as Verra, Gold Standard and BEE/ICM may be better buyers or partners than individual verifiers.
- A plausible wedge is "DataSnipper for GHG verification": evidence-to-figure tracing, re-performance of calculations with the deterministic engine, a findings log, and a report-completeness checker. The existing carbonTraceAI calculation engine and provenance store become the verifier's re-performance tool.

### Gaps
- No usage or pricing data for any verifier-side carbon tool.
- Unknown whether DataSnipper is used by TIC GHG verifiers in practice.
- No interviews or survey evidence of verifier willingness to pay.
- No information on SGS, TÜV or DNV in-house AI verification tools; one search found nothing for 2026.

## 4. Carbon consultants: market, pain, tools and consultant-led distribution

### Takeaway
A fragmented consultant layer already exists for CBAM and GHG work in India, Türkiye and Africa, priced low (Indian SMEs pay about ₹2 lakh/yr). White-label and partner programmes for consultants are an established distribution pattern in carbon software (Gaia, Green Project, Greenly Pro, Persefoni Pro). Consultant-led distribution is proven but crowded. A consultant platform is essentially the SME product sold through a channel. It does not escape SME price ceilings.

### Cited Findings
- A small Indian exporter pays about ₹2 lakh (~€2,000) a year for a CBAM consultant. — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/environment/how-the-eus-carbon-border-tax-is-reshaping-indian-msmes)
- Indian CBAM consultancies marketing measurement, verification preparation and registry reporting to exporters include Corpseed, GreenSutra, Sentra and CarbonSettle. MSMEs lack technical staff to interpret the rules. — [Corpseed](https://www.corpseed.com/service/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam-india); [GreenSutra](https://greensutra.in/services/cbam-solutions/); [Sentra](https://sentra.world/blogs/cbam/cbam-consultant-for-msmes/); [Down to Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/economy/eus-carbon-rules-enter-implementation-phase-putting-indian-msmes-under-pressure)
- Sentra advises starting verifier engagement in Q1 2026 because verifier capacity for Indian installations is still forming (vendor framing). — [Sentra](https://sentra.world/blogs/cbam/cbam-consultant-for-indian-exporters-before-2027/)
- Partner and white-label programmes:
  - Gaia offers full white-label (own logo, domain, colours), resale or API. — [Gaia partners](https://gaiacompany.io/carbon-accounting-software/partners/)
  - Green Project offers a white-label admin dashboard for consultancies and accountants, with margin-based resale. — [Green Project](https://greenprojecttech.com/newsroom/white-label-press-release)
  - Greenly launched Greenly Pro (Jan 2025) for consultants to resell and earn referral and resale margins. Sia Partners became a Greenly Gold partner. — [Sustainable Tech Partner](https://sustainabletechpartner.com/news/carbon-accounting-software-startup-greenly-launches-partner-enablement-software-services/); [Sia Partners](https://www.sia-partners.com/en/about-us/corporate-news/sia-partners-becomes-greenlys-gold-solution-partner)
  - Persefoni Pro Consultants Network licenses its platform to consultants and notes that software is "increasingly a requirement in client RFPs". — [Persefoni](https://persefoni.com/pro-consultant-program)
- Greenly's head of partnerships said many carbon vendors have yet to engage boutique consultants, VARs, MSPs and accounting firms. — [Sustainable Tech Partner](https://sustainabletechpartner.com/news/carbon-accounting-software-startup-greenly-launches-partner-enablement-software-services/)
- Workiva cites channel-partner momentum for its carbon module. — [Sustainable Tech Partner](https://sustainabletechpartner.com/news/workiva-carbon-accounting-software-shows-channel-partner-and-customer-momentum-ceo-asserts/)

### Inferences
- Consultant pains are well matched to agents: chasing client data, filling repetitive monitoring-plan and communication templates, mapping CN codes, and preparing verifier evidence packs. Consultants are already the de facto preparers. They are also the natural seller of a verifier-ready pack, because verifiers cannot advise.
- At about €2k per SME client per year, a consultant can pay perhaps 10-30% of that to tooling (my assumption), so €200-600 per client per year. Value comes from consultant leverage (more clients per FTE), not ARPU. This is the Basis/Harvey logic, but at emerging-market price points.
- Independence cuts both ways. A firm cannot be both consultant and verifier for the same client. Consultants and verifiers are therefore separate buyer pools, and a product could serve both sides of the same engagement (prepare, then verify), much as audit-client and auditor tools coexist (Workiva and DataSnipper).

### Gaps
- No market-size figure for CBAM or GHG consulting in India, Africa or Türkiye. Searches returned vendor pages only, with no fee surveys.
- No data on how many CBAM or GHG consultancies operate in each country, or on their tool stacks (Excel is the assumed baseline).
- No evidence yet of white-label agent platforms (as opposed to calculators) sold to carbon consultants.

## 5. Accreditation and independence constraints: what may be delegated to software

### Takeaway
Accreditation rules do not ban AI tools. They do ban delegating the conformity decision or opinion, and they make AI adoption a "significant change" the accreditation body must be told about. Verifiers remain responsible for validating tools. Impartiality rules bar the same body from consulting and verifying the same claim, so a vendor must keep verifier-side and preparer-side deployments separate. Agents can draft, trace, re-perform and flag. They cannot decide, sign or substitute for site visits.

### Cited Findings
- UKAS and DAkkS guidance on AI:
  - The joint UKAS–DAkkS AI bulletin (Mar 2026) says: "Final decisions on conformity may not be delegated to AI systems within the existing accreditation framework."
  - AI adoption is a significant change that must be communicated to the national accreditation body in a timely manner.
  - The CAB must assign responsibility for deciding that an AI system is fit for purpose and for its ongoing conformity.
  - The bulletins add no new requirements; the ISO/IEC standards remain authoritative.

  — [UKAS–DAkkS bulletin](https://www.ukas.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/UKAS49_U266-UKAS-Co-Branded-Ai-Bulletin-v2.pdf); [UKAS June 2025 bulletin](https://www.ukas.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2025.06.20-Technical-Bulletin-The-use-of-Artificial-Intelligence-AI-technologies-in-accredited-conformity-assessment.pdf); [DAkkS press](https://www.dakks.de/en/pressrelease/dakks-and-ukas-publish-joint-technical-bulletin-on-the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-ai.html); [UKAS Mar 2026 note](https://www.ukas.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026.03.06-Technical-Bulletin-Understanding-AI-in-accredited-conformity-assessment-AI-Technical-Bulletins.pdf)
- European Accreditation set up a Working Group on AI (co-convenors from TURKAK and ISRAC) at its 59th General Assembly to review practice and coordinate guidance. — [EA 59th GA](https://european-accreditation.org/highlights-of-the-59th-general-assembly/)
- EU AVR 2018/2067 Art. 43 forbids verification where there is an "unacceptable risk to impartiality". — [legislation.gov.uk](https://legislation.gov.uk/eur/2018/2067/article/43/2020-01-31?view=plain)
- The predecessor Regulation 600/2012 Art. 42 said a verifier "shall not outsource the independent review or the issuance of the verification report". It treated shared ownership, personnel or finances with a consultancy as compromising impartiality. I could not confirm the equivalent wording in 2018/2067. — [legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/600/article/42/data.html)
- ISO 14065:2020-based checklists ask whether the body avoids providing "both consultancy and validation/verification for the same claim from the same client". They also require self-review and familiarity threats to be managed. — [HKCAS 021](https://www.itc.gov.hk/en/quality/hkas/doc/hkcas/HKCAS021-A2A.pdf)
- TÜV NORD policy requires at least 2 years between consultancy and verification for the same GHG statement. — [TÜV NORD](https://tuv-nord.com/vn/vi/tai-lieu/tinh-doc-lap-va-tinh-khach-quan)
- Mandatory site visits (first CBAM year, then periodically) cannot be replaced by remote or AI review except in extraordinary circumstances. — [EUR-Lex 2025/2546](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32025R2546); [Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2752703-cbam-price-method-verification-drafts-leaked)
- VCS limits a VVB to 6 consecutive years per project, so engagement-history tracking is a compliance need. — [Verra VCS](https://verra.org/programs/verified-carbon-standard/)

### Inferences
- Allowed with controls (and NAB notification):
  - document ingestion and evidence tracing;
  - re-performance of calculations;
  - anomaly and outlier detection;
  - sampling-plan suggestions;
  - drafting the risk analysis, verification plan, findings log and report;
  - completeness checks against regulatory checklists.

  Not allowed: issuing the opinion, independent review, materiality conclusions, or site-visit substitution.
- A vendor selling the same AI to an operator (preparer) and its verifier creates a self-review perception risk. The issue is not legal independence, because the vendor is not the verifier. It is that a verifier using the same engine to check numbers that engine produced weakens professional scepticism. Mitigations: separate tenancy, independent re-computation paths, and disclosure. This is relevant because carbonTraceAI's existing product is operator-side.
- Tools that verifiers rely on must themselves be validated. Deterministic, reproducible calculation plus full provenance (carbonTraceAI's existing strength) is easier to get a NAB-accepted validation for than LLM outputs.
- Data security: verifiers handle confidential production data across competitors. Cross-client data pooling ("network effects") will face confidentiality clauses in verification contracts and accreditation confidentiality requirements, an ISO 17029/14065 norm (inference; exact clause not retrieved).

### Gaps
- Full text of the UKAS bulletin was not retrieved (HTTP 403). Principles beyond the quoted lines are unconfirmed.
- Whether ISSA 5000 contains specific requirements on automated tools and techniques: not found.
- The EU CBAM verification guidance's position on IT tools used by verifiers was not checked beyond "never mentions AI".
- No NAB (DAkkS, UKAS, NABCB, SANAS) decision accepting or rejecting a specific AI verification tool was found.

## 6. Viability: price points, buyer concentration, sales cycle, defensibility

### Takeaway
"AI for carbon verifiers" is a real but narrow, concentrated and slow wedge. The pain (a capacity shortage in 2026-27) and the budget (€5-50k+ fees per verification) are real. But most of the spend sits with about 10 TIC and Big-4 groups that build or acquire, and the long-tail VVBs are small. Data network effects are limited by confidentiality. The stronger near-term play is probably two-sided: a verifier-ready evidence pack and re-performance workspace sold via consultants and operators, with a free or cheap verifier seat. Selling directly to boutique VVBs and emerging-market verifiers (India ACVAs, local African bodies seeking CBAM accreditation via EA-6/05 cooperation) is a secondary channel. Registries and standards bodies (Isometric model) are partners.

### Cited Findings
- Fee pool per verification is €3k-150k+ depending on size (vendor estimates). — [cbamguide](https://cbamguide.com/importers/verification/); [iFactory](https://ifactoryapp.com/industries/steel-plant/cbam-verification-body-selection-guide-for-steel-exporters); [The Trade Hub](https://www.thetradehub.eu/en/guides/cbam/verification)
- Importer view: 10 medium-complexity installations cost about €120k-250k in first-cycle verification (vendor estimate). — [CBAM Tools](https://cbam-tools.com/blog/cbam-emissions-verification-guide-importers)
- The first CBAM accreditations went exclusively to large TIC groups and one specialist. — [cbamguide](https://cbamguide.com/compliance/accredited-verifiers/)
- BV is building or buying AI sustainability capability (Aligned Incentives acquisition). — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/bureau-veritas-acquires-aligned-incentives-to-bolster-scope-3-product-carbon-footprint-capabilities)
- The financial-audit analogue shows enterprise distribution via Big-4 firm-wide deals (DataSnipper with the Big 4; MindBridge with KPMG). — [Clay](https://www.clay.com/dossier/datasnipper-funding); [Peakspan](https://www.peakspancapital.com/resources/news/ai-for-auditors-startup-mindbridge-scores-global-deal-with-kpmg)
- Isometric shows AI review can sit inside a certifier or registry and raise venture capital ($40M Series A). — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/isometric-raises-40-million-to-expand-industrial-certification-platform-beyond-carbon-markets/)
- Verra is automating procedures internally and tightening VVB oversight (PMP, suspensions). A standards body pushing VVB quality creates a pull for VVB quality tooling. — [Verra](https://verra.org/?p=20438); [Verra PMP](https://verra.org/validation-verification/vvb-performance-monitoring-program-pmp/)

### Inferences
- **Price points (hypotheses, not sourced):**
  - Per-engagement pricing of about 3-10% of the verification fee (€300-3,000 per installation) aligns with a verifier's saved days.
  - TIC enterprise licences would be six figures but take 12-24 months to land.
  - Boutique VVBs or ACVAs at €5-20k per year.
- **Buyer concentration:** about 10 groups control most compliance verification. Losing or never winning one is material. TIC procurement, IT security review and NAB notification of the tool all lengthen the cycle. A realistic first-revenue cycle is 6-12+ months even for boutiques.
- **Timing risk:** the CBAM crunch (Q4 2026 to Sep 2027) is a one-off spike in first-year verifications. Verifiers won't adopt a new tool mid-crunch without NAB-notified validation. A startup may miss the peak and arrive for a steadier run-rate market (annual ETS/CBAM/CCTS cycles), which is still recurring.
- **Defensibility:**
  - Weak: data network effects across verifications (confidentiality).
  - Moderate: a library of regulation- and methodology-specific checklists, test suites and accepted evidence patterns (CBAM CN codes, CCTS, Verra methodologies).
  - Moderate to strong: a position as the shared evidence layer between operator, consultant and verifier, similar to Workiva/DataSnipper on both sides of the financial audit.
- **Critical view:** the Harvey/Basis analogy is imperfect. Law and accounting have hundreds of thousands of billable professionals. Carbon verification likely has thousands, so the TAM is small unless the product also covers corporate sustainability assurance under ISSA 5000/CSRD/SB 253. There, Big-4 audit firms (55% share) will mostly use DataSnipper, Workiva or in-house tools. The emerging-market verifier and consultant segment is the least served, but has the lowest willingness to pay.

### Gaps
- No primary evidence of verifier willingness to pay, procurement process or tooling budgets; discovery interviews are needed.
- No data on what share of a verifier's man-days is desk review that software could compress (vs. site, travel and review).
- No benchmarks for software spend as a share of TIC sustainability-service revenue.
